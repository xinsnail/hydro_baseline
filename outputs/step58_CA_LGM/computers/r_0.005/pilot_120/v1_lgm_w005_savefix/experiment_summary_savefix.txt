Step58 corrected 120-epoch paired pilot: HyDRO vs HyDRO+LGM V1

Validity correction
- The earlier V1 result (51.10 +/- 4.19) is invalid for method comparison.
- Its checkpoint stored the normalized GCN propagation matrix as though it were
  the raw condensed adjacency, so the evaluator processed the graph in the
  wrong representation.
- Training now uses the normalized matrix internally and serializes the raw PGE
  adjacency, matching the HyDRO baseline and evaluator contract.

Protocol
- Dataset: Computers
- Reduction rate: 0.005
- Condensation seed: 1
- Condensation epochs: 120
- Checkpoints: epochs 60 and 120, selected only by validation Macro-F1
- Final LP runs: 10 paired initialization runs
- V1 intervention: positive/negative link gradient matching in PGE phases
- LGM weight: 0.05
- RankGM: disabled
- LP-aware spectral modification: disabled

Decision
- The corrected V1 does not collapse the propagation structure.
- Its mean Macro-F1 is +0.25 pp above the paired HyDRO pilot, but the 95% CI
  crosses zero and only 6/10 runs improve; this is a positive but unconfirmed
  pilot signal, not evidence of a stable improvement.
- The old 51.10 result and the old 94.99/218.51 percent collapse claims must not
  be used as evidence against CA-LGM.
- A conflict-aware follow-up is justified, but it must be compared with this
  corrected V1 rather than with the invalid checkpoint.
