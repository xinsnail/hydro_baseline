Step58 corrected V1 fixed-weight CA-LGM: 600-epoch analysis

Protocol
- Dataset: Computers
- Reduction rate: 0.005
- Condensation seed: 1
- Condensation epochs: 600
- Validation checkpoints: every 60 epochs
- Final LP runs: 10 paired initialization runs
- CA-LGM weight: 0.05
- Link objective: positive and negative gradient direction plus magnitude
- PCGrad: disabled
- Dynamic gradient limiting: disabled
- RankGM: disabled

Results
- HyDRO test Macro-F1: 73.57 +/- 1.52 percent
- V1.1 gradient-safe CA-LGM: 73.41 +/- 0.71 percent
- Corrected fixed-weight V1: 74.25 +/- 0.80 percent

Corrected V1 versus HyDRO
- Mean difference: +0.68 percentage points
- Paired wins: 5/10
- Paired 95% CI: [-0.17, +1.53] percentage points
- Paired t-test p-value: 0.103
- Standard deviation reduction: 47.2 percent
- Correlation between HyDRO score and paired delta: -0.857
- The mean improvement is promising but is not confirmed statistically on one
  condensed graph.

Corrected V1 versus V1.1
- Mean difference: +0.84 percentage points
- Paired wins: 9/10
- Paired 95% CI: [+0.25, +1.42] percentage points
- Paired t-test p-value: 0.010
- PCGrad plus 2-percent limiting materially reduced the useful task signal.

Loss-scale diagnostics (not gradient-scale diagnostics)
- PGE intervention updates: 1200
- Mean weighted-LGM/node-GM loss ratio: 1.096
- Median weighted-LGM/node-GM loss ratio: 0.690
- Maximum weighted-LGM/node-GM loss ratio: 4.737
- This ratio must not be interpreted as a gradient-norm ratio.

Decision
- Fixed-weight V1 is the current Step58 candidate because it improves mean F1
  by 0.68 pp over HyDRO and clearly outperforms V1.1.
- It has not yet established generality: the paired CI against HyDRO crosses
  zero and only one condensation seed has been evaluated.
- Freeze weight=0.05 and all protocol choices. Do not add RankGM, PCGrad, a
  propagation budget, or tune the weight on seed 1.
- The next experiment is external confirmation on condensation seeds 2 and 3
  with their paired HyDRO baselines. Only after that should Photo/Cora or LAPT
  composition be considered.
