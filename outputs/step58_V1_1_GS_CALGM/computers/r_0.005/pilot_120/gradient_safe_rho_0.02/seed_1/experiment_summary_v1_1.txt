Step58-V1.1 120-epoch paired pilot: gradient-safe CA-LGM

Protocol
- Dataset: Computers
- Reduction rate: 0.005
- Condensation seed: 1
- Condensation epochs: 120
- Checkpoints: epochs 60 and 120, selected only by validation Macro-F1
- Final LP runs: 10 paired initialization runs
- Link objective: positive/negative direction-only CA-LGM
- Conflict control: PCGrad projection of link gradient against HyDRO gradient
- Effective link-gradient ratio: rho=0.02 at every PGE update
- Maximum scale: 5.0
- RankGM: disabled
- Propagation-budget penalty: disabled

Interpretation and decision
- V1.1 safely injects link-task gradients without materially disrupting HyDRO
  propagation and produces a paired positive pilot result against HyDRO.
- The 10 LP-run paired CI excludes zero, and variance is lower than both HyDRO
  and corrected V1 under this single condensed graph.
- This is not yet a general method claim: only Computers, R=0.005, condensation
  seed 1, and 120 condensation epochs have been tested.
- The correct next gate is a 600-epoch seed-1 comparison, followed by new
  condensation seeds and other datasets only if that gate remains positive.
