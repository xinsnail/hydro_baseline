Decision
- V1.1 does not improve the 600-epoch mean Macro-F1 and fails the predeclared
  gate of positive mean gain with at least 7/10 paired wins.
- It should not proceed directly to condensation seeds 2 and 3 as an F1
  improvement method.
- The result supports a variance-compression interpretation: weak LP runs are
  improved, but strong LP runs are reduced.
- The next clean test is the corrected fixed-weight V1 at 600 epochs, because
  that branch retained more task-gradient information and was positive at the
  120-epoch pilot. No new module or test-guided hyperparameter search should be
  added before that comparison.
